# Travel-inconvenience claim conditions

The language used when comparing travel-inconvenience insurance (旅遊不便險) products from several insurers: what each one pays, under what circumstances, and how those terms relate to each other.

## Language

### Policy text

**Product** (商品):
One insurer's travel-inconvenience policy. It is the unit being compared.
_Avoid_: Policy, insurer, plan

**Clause** (條款):
A numbered unit of policy text, such as 「第十二條」. It is the unit that is cited as evidence.
_Avoid_: Article, provision, policy condition

**Condition** (理賠條件):
The smallest unit that independently decides one payout: a covered event, a threshold, a benefit type (fixed amount or reimbursement), a limit, and its exclusions. One Clause can contain several Conditions.
_Avoid_: Clause, policy condition (保單條件), rule

**Wording version** (條款版本):
Which edition of the regulator's reference clauses a Product's text follows: old (in force from 2022-09-01) or new (in force from 2026-04-01).
_Avoid_: Generation, edition, revision

**Plan** (方案):
A named tier of Benefit amounts sold under a Product, such as an economy tier and a premium tier.
_Avoid_: Tier, package, 計畫

**Benefit amount** (保險金額):
The amount a Condition pays under a given Plan. It is stated on the policy schedule, not in the Clause.
_Avoid_: Payout, sum insured, limit

**Aggregate limit** (合計上限):
A cap, stated in a Clause, on the total paid across several Conditions.
_Avoid_: Shared limit, cap

### Relationships between Conditions

**Alignment** (對齊):
The relationship between Conditions that cover the same kind of event but differ in their parameters, either across Products or across Wording versions. An example is a typhoon-warning exclusion that exists in the new wording and not in the old.
_Avoid_: Overlap, comparison

**Overlap** (重疊):
The relationship between two or more Conditions in the same Product that are triggered by the same event, so the event is paid more than once. An Overlap under an Aggregate limit is already resolved by the policy.
_Avoid_: Alignment, dependency, duplication

### Judging

**Scenario** (情境):
A described travel incident, with the facts needed to judge it, that is judged against every Product.
_Avoid_: Question, claim, case

**Cause** (事故原因):
Why the incident in a Scenario happened, such as weather, mechanical failure, a strike, or the insured's own fault. How a Cause is classified can change the Verdict.
_Avoid_: Dependency (相依), reason

**Verdict** (判定):
The outcome of one Scenario against one Product: paid, not paid, or undetermined. An undetermined Verdict always states why: the Cause is ambiguous, the Scenario lacks a fact, or the Clauses are silent.
_Avoid_: Answer, decision, escalation
